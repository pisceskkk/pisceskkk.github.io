vLLM is an open-source inference and serving engine for large language models, focused on high throughput, memory efficiency, and production serving ergonomics.

My work around vLLM focuses on distributed inference and long-context performance, including sequence parallelism and model adaptation work used in vLLM-based serving stacks.
