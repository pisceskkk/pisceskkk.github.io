vLLM-Ascend brings vLLM inference and serving support to Ascend hardware through a community-maintained hardware plugin.

My work focuses on Ascend inference optimization, including sequence parallelism, distributed inference, DeepSeek model adaptation, and long-context performance tuning in vLLM-Ascend environments.
