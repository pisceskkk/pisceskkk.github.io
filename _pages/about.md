I am an AI Engineer at Huawei's Ascend Inference Development Department, working on large language model inference optimization, including sequence parallelism and distributed inference for vLLM & vLLM-Ascend. I received my Master's degree in Computer Science from the [Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://www.ict.ac.cn/) (ICT/UCAS), advised by Prof. [Lin Gao](https://geometrylearning.com/). My research focused on 3D computer vision and computer graphics, particularly audio-driven talking face generation and real-time 3D head avatar synthesis.

Previously, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence & Big Data) from the [National University of Defense Technology](https://www.nudt.edu.cn/) (NUDT), where I was actively involved in competitive programming and ranked 4th out of 94 students.
